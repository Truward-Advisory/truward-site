Privacy Policy

How Truward collects, uses, and protects information from visitors to this site.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Truward ("Truward," "we," "us," or "our") operates truwardadvisory.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site, why we collect it, and what choices you have. It applies to this website only, not to any services we may deliver to clients under a separate engagement agreement.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways: information you give us directly, and information collected automatically as you browse.

Information you provide. When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect your name, organization, email address, and whatever message you write. We use this solely to respond to your inquiry.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, this site may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use it and to measure the effectiveness of advertising. Depending on the choice you make in the cookie banner, this may include analytics tools and Google Ads conversion tracking, which tells us that a form submission followed an ad click, without identifying the specific ad or campaign to us in a personally identifiable way.

How we use information

  • To respond to messages submitted through the contact form
  • To understand which pages and content are useful to visitors
  • To measure whether advertising campaigns are leading to inquiries
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of the site

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond running this site and following up on inquiries.

Cookies and advertising

This site may use cookies for two purposes: essential site functionality, and — only if you accept them in the cookie banner — analytics and advertising, including Google Ads conversion tracking. If you decline, only strictly necessary cookies are used and no advertising or analytics tags are loaded. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site and reloading the page.

If Google Ads conversion tracking is active, Google may set its own cookies subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can control how Google uses this data through Google's Ads Settings.

Third parties we work with

We use a small number of third-party services to run this site. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and delivers them to our inbox. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages / Cloudflare — host and deliver this website. Standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be processed by these providers as part of normal web hosting.
  • Google Ads — if enabled, provides conversion tracking so we can measure ad performance. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, and delete or anonymize them when no longer needed. You can request earlier deletion at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the information we hold about you, and the right to object to or restrict certain processing. If you are in the EU or UK, these rights arise under the GDPR; if you are a California resident, similar rights arise under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its sale or sharing — we do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information submitted through this site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our use of tools like advertising and analytics changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or how your information is handled can be sent to hello@truwardadvisory.com.
